出典：DVB　2026年9月26日（土）
The United League of Arakan (ULA), the political wing of the アラカン軍 (AA), announced the establishment of the 15-member “Arakha People’s Media Council” on Wednesday. Aung Marm Oo, the Editor-in-Chief of Development Media Group (DMG), has been appointed to chair the newly-formed council. According to the AA, the council was formed to protect the public’s right to access accurate information, defend press freedom and the rights of journalists, mediate and resolve media-related disputes, and carry out media development initiatives across AA-administered regions. The 15-member council includes eight representatives from the media sector, two from literature and arts, and one each representing youth affairs, women’s affairs, legal affairs, finance, and statistics.
