Founded by Dr. Cynthia Maung in 1989 following ミャンマー’s ‘8888 Uprising,’ the clinic provides essential healthcare, education, and protection services to internally displaced persons (IDPs), refugees, and migrant workers along the Thai–ミャンマー border.
